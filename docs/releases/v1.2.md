# v1.2 (2026-09-16)

Compared current main `1dc6a8d` with `3a5dfe7`, the source checkout recorded in the
[v1.1 release notes](v1.1.md).

- Strengthened general-search evidence review to preserve the original question, source conditions,
  and supported partial answers without guessing at missing facts.
- Added guidance to keep Medicare product eligibility rules separate and require source quotes
  for qualifications, without treating enrollment timing or catalog availability as eligibility.
- Tightened Essential Extras attribution to require supporting source quotes and clarify that
  retrieved benefits may be incomplete.
- Added guidance to preserve coverage exclusions and attach each cost to its stated service and
  setting, omitting amounts whose attribution is unclear.
- Added clarification for unavailable plans before searching, preserving the shopper's topic
  without substituting similarly named plans.
- Normalized recognized single-plan selections with descriptive prefixes to the catalog name
  while retaining the original question.
- Updated enrollment-assistance messaging to direct members to Health Plan Advisors or scheduled
  appointments, with revised callback wording for prospects.

Full evaluation against v1.1 found no regressions.
